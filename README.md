## 🍵 Key Features & 3D Animations

### 1. Scene 1: The Perfect Tea Drop (Zen Tea Atelier)
- **3D Kyusu Teapot**: Handcrafted Japanese ceramic celadon teapot with traditional wood side-handle.
- **Falling Droplet Physics**: Single tea drop falling from the spout, landing into the ceramic cup, bouncing with a realistic rebound bead.
- **Concentric Liquid Ripples**: Dynamic expanding wave rings on the liquid surface upon impact.
- **Volumetric Steam & Floating Leaves**: Semi-transparent drifting steam particles and 3D green tea leaves levitating gracefully around the cup.
- **Controls**: "Drop Tea Bead", "Continuous Stream" pour mode, Slow-motion, and 4 Origin tea blends.

### 2. Scene 2: Artisan Coffee Craft (Third-Wave Pour-Over)
- **3D Gooseneck Kettle & V60 Dripper**: Matte black and brushed brass kettle pouring into a ceramic cone dripper and double-walled glass carafe.
- **Bean Drop Simulation**: Glossy roasted coffee beans tumbling into the grinder/dripper.
- **Crema Swirl & Extraction**: Rich dark espresso liquid filling the server with golden-brown crema and swirling micro-bubbles.
- **Aroma Motes**: Golden steam and warmth particles rising from the brew.

### 3. Scene 3: Steaming Rice & Tea Ready Feast (The Hearth)
- **Authentic Japanese Donburi Bowl**: Textured indigo-black stoneware bowl filled with glistening pearl rice grains, roasted black sesame, and crisp nori seaweed strips.
- **Lifting Lid Animation**: The bowl's ceramic and gold finial lid lifts up, tilts, and floats aside upon command.
- **Billowing Steam & Celebration**: Massive hot steam cloud bursts from the opened bowl, accompanied by a side cup of steaming hot ceremonial tea, floating dark walnut chopsticks, and a golden celebration sparkle cascade.
- **Celebration Banner & Chime**: Rings a soothing pentatonic chime and displays the celebratory *"ITADAKIMASU! Rice & Ceremonial Tea Are Ready!"* toast.

## 🎵 Coffee Shop Lo-Fi Music & Barista Ambience Engine
- **Procedural Lo-Fi Jazz Piano**: Plays warm Rhodes electric piano chord progressions (Dm9 -> G13 -> Cmaj9 -> Am7) synthesized natively with the Web Audio API (zero external `.mp3` files needed).
- **Vinyl Crackle & Tape Hiss**: Authentic warm vinyl dust crackle for a cozy cafe atmosphere.
- **Organic Barista Sounds**: Gentle ceramic cup clinks and subtle distant steam hiss puffs happening organically every 8-15 seconds.
- **Ambience Mixer**: Custom volume sliders in the header for Lo-Fi Piano, Vinyl Warmth, and Barista Clinks.
